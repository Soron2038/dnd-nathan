Verbindliche Fassung für diese Kampagne.

> **Achtung, häufige 2014-Regression:** In der 2014er Fassung sind Goblins und Bugbears
> `Humanoid (Goblinoid)` mit Alignment `neutral evil` / `chaotic evil`, der Goblin hat 7 HP und
> AC 15, der Bugbear `Brute` und `Surprise Attack` mit Morningstar. **All das gilt 2024 nicht mehr.**
> 2024 sind Goblinoids **Fey**, die Werte und Actions sind andere. Im Zweifel diese Datei verwenden.

## Gemeinsame 2024-Änderungen gegenüber 2014

- Goblins und Bugbears sind **Fey (Goblinoid)**, keine Humanoids. Sie stammen aus dem Feywild.
- Goblins sind **Chaotic Neutral**, Bugbears **Chaotic Evil**.
- Goblin-Angriffe erhalten Zusatzschaden, **wenn der attack roll Advantage hatte** (nicht via Sneak-Attack-Logik).
- Der Bugbear hat kein `Brute` und keinen `Surprise Attack` mehr, dafür `Abduct`, `Grab` und `Light Hammer`.
- Lore: Goblins sind „Feywild embodiments of recklessness and ruin", die sich an Zerstörung erfreuen —
  mutwilliges Verwüsten und das Quälen von Vieh sind ausdrücklich typisch.

## Goblin Minion

- Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral — Initiative +2 (12)
- **AC 12**, **HP 7 (2d6)**, Speed 30 ft.
- Str 8 (−1) · Dex 15 (+2) · Con 10 (+0) · Int 10 (+0) · Wis 8 (−1) · Cha 8 (−1); Saves = Mods
- Skills Stealth +6; Gear Daggers (3)
- Senses Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9; Languages Common, Goblin
- **CR 1/8** (XP 25; PB +2)

**Actions** — *Dagger.* Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft.
Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) Piercing damage.

**Bonus Actions** — *Nimble Escape.* The goblin takes the Disengage or Hide action.

## Goblin Warrior

- Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral — Initiative +2 (12)
- **AC 15**, **HP 10 (3d6)**, Speed 30 ft.
- Str 8 (−1) · Dex 15 (+2) · Con 10 (+0) · Int 10 (+0) · Wis 8 (−1) · Cha 8 (−1); Saves = Mods
- Skills Stealth +6; Gear Leather Armor, Scimitar, Shield, Shortbow

**Actions**

- *Scimitar.* Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing damage,
  **plus 2 (1d4) Slashing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.**
- *Shortbow.* Ranged Attack Roll: +4, range 80/320 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage,
  **plus 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the attack roll had Advantage.**

**Bonus Actions** — *Nimble Escape.* The goblin takes the Disengage or Hide action.

## Goblin Boss

- Small Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Neutral — Initiative +2 (12)
- **AC 17**, **HP 21 (6d6)**, Speed 30 ft.
- Str 10 (+0) · Dex 15 (+2) · Con 10 (+0) · Int 10 (+0) · Wis 8 (−1) · Cha 10 (+0)
- Skills Stealth +6; Gear Chain Shirt, Scimitar, Shield, Shortbow
- Senses Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 9; Languages Common, Goblin
- **CR 1** (XP 200; PB +2)

**Actions**

- *Multiattack.* Two attacks, using Scimitar or Shortbow in any combination.
- *Scimitar.* Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Slashing, plus 2 (1d4) if the roll had Advantage.
- *Shortbow.* Ranged Attack Roll: +4, range 80/320 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing, plus 2 (1d4) if the roll had Advantage.

**Bonus Actions** — *Nimble Escape.* Disengage or Hide.

**Reactions** — *Redirect Attack.* Trigger: a creature the goblin can see makes an attack roll against it.
Response: the goblin chooses a Medium or Small ally within 5 feet; they swap places and the ally becomes
the target instead.

## Bugbear Warrior

- Medium Fey (Goblinoid), Chaotic Evil — Initiative +2 (12)
- **AC 14**, **HP 33 (6d8 + 6)**, Speed 30 ft.
- Str 15 (+2) · Dex 14 (+2) · Con 13 (+1) · Int 8 (−1) · Wis 11 (+0) · Cha 9 (−1)
- Skills Stealth +6, Survival +2; Gear Hide Armor, Light Hammers (3)
- Senses Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10; Languages Common, Goblin
- **CR 1** (XP 200; PB +2)

**Traits** — *Abduct.* The bugbear needn't spend extra movement to move a creature it is grappling.

**Actions**

- *Grab.* Melee Attack Roll: +4, **reach 10 ft.** Hit: 9 (2d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is
  Medium or smaller, it has the **Grappled** condition (escape DC 12).
- *Light Hammer.* Melee or Ranged Attack Roll: +4 (**with Advantage if the target is Grappled by the
  bugbear**), reach 10 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 9 (3d4 + 2) Bludgeoning damage.
